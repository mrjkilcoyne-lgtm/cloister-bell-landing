Thesis

Why this works without training.

1. Signal is not the bottleneck.

The available bandwidth across the three off-the-shelf sensor classes already exceeds the entropy of a small command vocabulary. The bottleneck is the map, not the data.

2. Bridges are isomorphisms, not models.

A bridge is a formal mapping between a class of physiological events and a class of symbolic intents. Because the mapping is mathematical it transfers across users by construction. There is no model to retrain. There is no enrolment.

3. The realiser is the LLM at the end.

Once intent is decoded into the symbolic grammar, the LLM at the end of the pipe expands it into language, action, or query. The LLM is replaceable. The bridges are not. That is the whole point.

4. What v1 ships.

Five bridges. A reference Android app. A spec other teams can implement against. Source under a permissive licence. Bridge names and the reference vocabulary will be published on launch, not before.
