Earbuds

Watch

A wrist sensor with PPG, accelerometer and gyroscope. Optical heart-rate is the carrier; the IMU is the gesture channel.

Ring

A ring with SpO2, infrared and IMU. The ring is the privacy gate — remove it and the decoder stops.

Bluetooth LE host, on-device decoder, optional cloud realiser. The decoder runs locally; biosignal never leaves the body unless the user sends it.
